Our story

How it started

Ecommerce began in a small studio with three sewing machines and a simple frustration: most basics were either overpriced or fell apart after a few washes. We spent a year testing fabrics before releasing our first t-shirt.

Today we still design everything ourselves, work with the same small production partners, and ship every order from a warehouse we can walk through in five minutes.

What we care about

Honest materials

We name the fabric on every product page — no vague "premium blend" labels.

Made to last

Every style goes through a wash-and-wear test before it's approved for release.

Fair pricing

We sell direct, skip the wholesale markup, and pass the difference on to you.
